When a source can't be added (three cases, shown in the Add card)
✕ That address isn't a source
It answered, but with a web page, not a list of apps. Check you copied the whole address.
✕ Couldn't reach it
Nothing answered at apps.bob.example. It may be offline, or the address may be wrong.
Try again later, or check the address with the publisher.
✕ Its signature doesn't check out
The list of apps wasn't signed by the key it claims. Someone may have tampered with it,
so the Nodo won't use it. Tell the publisher.
Each case appears in place of the source's details; the address stays filled in to fix and Check again.
