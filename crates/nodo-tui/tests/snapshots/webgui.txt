       The same pages as here, in a browser on your home network.
       You log in with the device passphrase. A login ends after 15 minutes without use.
       It is never on the internet or on Tor, and it is off until you turn it on here.

       ▲  While it is on, anyone on your home network can try the device passphrase at its
       login page. Until now that took an SSH key.
       ▲  Wrong tries are slowed down, up to a minute each. The pages can add SSH keys, so the
       passphrase alone then leads to a shell.
