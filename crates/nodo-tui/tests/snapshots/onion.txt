┌ About onion addresses ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ● An onion address is reached through Tor only. It never shows your IP and needs no open port.   │
│ ● The node's address serves only its restricted RPC, which wallets use. Never the full RPC.      │
│ ● Admin paths of sites are blocked on onion addresses. Keys are kept in backups.                 │
